Hi, I am **Puyu Zhou**, a fourth-year PhD candidate in **Artificial Intelligence** at the **Faculty of Innovation Engineering, Macau University of Science and Technology**.

Previously, I received an **MSc in Management** from the **School of Business, Macau University of Science and Technology**. My background connects technical research with cross-domain problem solving, and I am particularly interested in building research that is both methodologically solid and practically extensible.

My research focuses on **Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF)**, **3D reconstruction**, and **cross-domain machine learning**. More broadly, I am interested in neural scene representation, editable neural rendering, and applying machine learning methods across domains such as healthcare, economics, and interdisciplinary creative applications.

I am currently **actively seeking postdoctoral opportunities**. If my work aligns with your group’s interests, I would be very happy to connect.

For research collaborations, postdoctoral opportunities, or general inquiries, please reach out by email.
